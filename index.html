The idea

A small red dot can become a life-changing opportunity.

Red Dot Drive is a fundraising campaign by Youth Dreamers Foundation to create a scholarship and support programme for girl students who have the ability and ambition to pursue higher education but face financial barriers.

Every contribution helps move one more red dot from an idea to an opportunity.
