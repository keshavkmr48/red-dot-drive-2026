The cause

What your donation funds

Red Dot Drive 2026 is a campaign collecting funds to support government school girl students in Bihar through scholarships. Every girl deserves access to quality education that empowers her with a strong voice, equal opportunities and a brighter future.

Funds collected until the campaign closes go toward scholarships and support for elementary and higher education. Organisations and students are screened before being supported — this isn't a blind pass-through.

We're documenting the drive publicly — this page's progress bar updates as donations come in, and we'll share where every rupee lands once the drive closes.
